Terms of Service & Code Ownership Standard

EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026 • HOUSTON, TX • REVISION 2.4

1. 100% Client Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Upon final project delivery and milestone payment, 100% of the custom software source code, database architectures, migrations, API connectors, and styling created specifically for your company by Arx Tessera become your exclusive corporate property. Arx Tessera retains zero equity, levies zero recurring per-seat user toll fees, and will never hold your operational data hostage.

2. Fixed-Scope Statements of Work (SOW)

Every build begins with an upfront, written Statement of Work defining the exact business workflows to be replaced, schemas to be migrated, delivery milestones, and flat-rate investment. We do not bill ambiguous hourly overages or surprise scope fees.

3. The 90-Day Bug-Free Warranty & Parallel Launch Guarantee

All production deployments include a complimentary 90-day post-launch warranty covering bug fixes, latency optimizations, and database adjustments. Under our Zero-Disruption Parallel Launch Guarantee, custom tools are built and tested alongside your legacy software until your crew verifies every customer record and approves full cutover.

4. Hosting & Private Infrastructure

Systems are provisioned inside your company’s own cloud infrastructure (such as AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, or Hetzner VPS). You maintain direct root ownership and administrative access to all underlying cloud servers, databases, and encryption keys.
